Hello dear reader, welcome to my website!

There are three main characters in this website, Me (the writer), You (the reader) and This (the website).

To simplify things for further reference I will give each of us numbers:

I am number 1.
You are number 2.
This is number 3.

Intuitively, you can think of these as following the linguistic first, second, and third pronouns. Seems simple at first right? What's interesting here you may ask...

The trick, however,is to set this analogus to a mathematical field of size 3.
Here, This becomes number 0 and if we want, we can represent You as -1.
Again, you may ask, why would we do this?
Well, I may respond, it makes it actually very intuitive to think about what it is that is actually going on this website:

You becomes -1.
This becomes 0.
I remain 1.

Well then I hope things start becoming more clear.
I am somehow using this website to get to you!
That is, This is in between You and I.

To put it even more plainly, You become my opposite.
I will then venture to say, that as you embark on this journey with me, there will always be some a dance going on between Me and You. The reason I am laying this all out right now is that it is important for understanding how this website and my world view are organized.
