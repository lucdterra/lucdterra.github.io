Dear World,
I seem to have destroyed much of life by trying to destroy my life! Crazy, I know. My constant insatisfaction with both everything created by me and everything created by others has lead to me down one path: Destroy! Destroy! Destroy! Whenever I get the slightest bit of progress in anything but notice that things are either getting a bit too annoying or just too much, I just essentially give up and scrap. Whether that means getting out of class I don't like or a course I find I am too behind to fully grasp, my default answer for, well, the last two years almost has been to quit. I've become a serious quitter. The worst part is I know I've become a serious quitter and so my attempts to correct this often go in the other extreme: I'll force myself to do something I really don't want to do and suffer either the mental or bodily consequences of such and go back to my default quitting/deleting mentality. Even now, I must say it feels bad to say I've become a quitter. This is because the idea of destruction coming from me feels good. It feels as though it is the other that is the problem, not me. The thing being destroyed deserves being destroyed, not me. The odd thing is that this also applies when it comes to me. That is, somehow it feels good destroying myself because it feels almost as though I removing a part of myself I don't like, in many senses, purifying myself. This of course doesn't work, as I never fix the underlying issue and just push it away. I elude myself that the problem is gone rather than doing anything about it. Destruction feels easy, it's sexy, it never actually works. Shows how little understanding of chemistry I have: Nothing can be destroyed. Things are only dispersed I guess. So what is the solution? Well, ... Patience. I think. I am honestly not quite sure. Anyways, thank you for listening, well, reading.

p.s. sorry to everyone that I abandoned in these years of crisis.
