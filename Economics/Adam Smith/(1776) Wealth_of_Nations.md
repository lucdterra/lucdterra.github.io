# Wealth of Nations

## Book 1: On the Causes and Distribution of Production

### Chapter 6: On the Component Parts of the Price of Commodities

It seems as though land is what is ultimately necessarily for something to be produced, or better, "transformed", extracted or my favourite, **moved**.

Say you want to get sand out of a beach. 
Well then the first thing you need ,_by definition_, is a beach, preferably I guess one that has sand.
Hmm, I guess in this case the _definition_ is the end product you are trying to get to, in this case _beach sand_.
Then I think all the individual grains of sand would be all the _examples_ of such definition.
Hmm, but then, obviously, all the grains of sand on the beach don't make up the beach. 
Somehow beach sand is a definition contained inside a larger definition.

However if you were to try to do this by hand, good luck!
You might spend a whole life time and not get enough for whatever job you are trying to accomplish.

What I am trying to say here is that it seems like you would really need some sort of technology (tool), whether its as basic as shovel, as powerful as a digger, you need something specialized, something designed for your task.
This, tool, is what I call capital. 
It is never "strictly necessary" for the operation, but always a sort of enhancer of the work.
That is why it also represents the variable part of production and typically wants a say in the outcome.
I would almost venture to say these are _theorems_, in the sense that they are used by others in typically specialized ways to speed up proofs.

Lastly, of course, we have you dear reader.
You are a generalist, always have been and always will be. 
That is why to perform any one task you must always require the help of a specialist.

Wait, I'm getting off topic.

You are the mover, the labourer. 
You are trying to get sand from the beach, to say, a warehouse.
You like moving things but you need help and something to move.

If I were to continue with the math analogy here, labour is _exercise_.
Then, performing labour is solving an exercise.
